# Olim Programs, Credentials, and Non-Tech Fields

Last verified: November 2026. Programs, vouchers, and offices change often; always confirm on the official page before telling a user something is available. Not legal advice.

## Who helps with what

### Ministry of Aliyah and Integration (Misrad HaAliyah VeHaKlita, MOIA)
- Handles olim rights: Sal Klita (absorption basket), Hebrew ulpan entitlement, degree evaluation (for aliyah on or after 1 Jan 2025), absorption advisors, and various employment and training offers. The Ministry has an Employment Division with services for olim; the English gov.il page could not be loaded during research, so verify its current offerings directly (gov.il, search "Ministry of Aliyah and Integration employment").
- Training and vouchers: NBN's Upgrade page says some courses may be eligible for a Ministry voucher; check eligibility and amounts at gov.il and with your absorption advisor. (UNVERIFIED amounts; no figure is stated here.)
- Employer incentives and tech programs: the Innovation Authority's Human Capital Fund works with the Ministry on tech placement for olim. Example: an "Attracting Top AI Talent: New Olim and Returning Residents" call for proposals (opened 3 Aug 2026, closed 17 Sep 2026) funds organizations that place experienced AI professionals in Israeli companies. Individuals do not apply directly; ask NBN or the Innovation Authority which placement organizations are running programs. Eligibility in that call required a relevant degree (EE, CS, data/AI) and extensive experience; Hebrew ulpan and extra support may accompany placement.
- Contact: Ministry of Aliyah and Integration call center (check gov.il for the current number); NBN post-aliyah helpline (*3680 per NBN pages).

### Nefesh B'Nefesh (NBN)
- NBN assists North American and UK olim with aliyah logistics and post-arrival support. Current employment offerings per NBN pages (Nov 2026): employment guidance by the NBN Employment team (career transfer, early career, employee rights, entrepreneurship); an Israel Job Board (advertised as 1,000+ jobs from 280+ companies); NBN Recruitment Services (submit a CV; fields listed include high tech, CPA, sales/SDR, marketing, corporate law, office management); "Upgrade Yourself" short courses (tech, sales, marketing, finance, project management, freelancing; several priced at NIS 250-500 per NBN's page; schedules vary) with partner bootcamps; and for medical professionals, the International Medical Aliyah Program (IMAP).
- NBN does not become your employer or run payroll; a vendor article describes "career continuation" via employer of record (see `remote-for-us-employers.md`); it is a third-party description, not an NBN program (UNVERIFIED as an NBN offering).
- Pages to start with: https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/employment/ and https://www.nbn.org.il/jobboard and https://www.nbn.org.il/upgrade/
- Other organizations that assist olim employment: the Jewish Agency, AACI (Association of Americans and Canadians in Israel) in some cities, Tehila, Telem and Naale (see below), municipality absorption offices, and olim associations by origin country. Check what each currently offers.

### Naale and Telem
- Naale (Na'ale Elite Academy): a program for teens from abroad who study in Israeli high schools; relevant to families, not to job search.
- Telem: not a single stable job program; use "Telem" to ask the Ministry or Jewish Agency about "Telem" academic/professional initiatives. This skill could not verify a current Telem employment program for olim; do not state one exists without checking.
- Do not tell users these are job-placement programs unless the official page confirms.

### Israel Employment Service (Sherut HaTaasuka)
Government employment offices with vacancy lists and counselling, mostly Hebrew; some offices have olim-focused staff. Recent data cited in the press: about 15,000 tech job seekers and 18,000 tech vacancies registered (Aug 2026), i.e., the registered mismatch is about skills, not just counts.

## Degree recognition

Two different things:
1. Academic admission (a university deciding whether to admit you) is decided by each university, not by the Ministry.
2. Employment/salary recognition (Ishur/equivalency) for employers and public-sector ranks.

Per Nefesh B'Nefesh (which summarizes the official process):
- Aliyah on or after 1 Jan 2025: apply through your personal area on gov.il to the Ministry of Aliyah and Integration; the result (Ishur) should be accepted by public and private employers; the page states an expected turnaround of about 45 working days.
- Aliyah before 1 Jan 2025: apply to the Ministry of Education (degree evaluation): "equivalency" (public-sector ranking/salary) or "general approval."
- Documents: certified true copies (an absorption advisor can stamp them free per NBN); transcripts from every institution; pre-2000 high-school diploma requirements; note special rules for online degrees (online health, paramedical, and psychology degrees are likely not recognized) and non-academic credits.
- Most private employers (tech included) care about skills and experience, but visa, public-sector, teaching, and regulated roles require recognition. Council for Higher Education (CHE, Mo'etzet HaHashkala HaGvohah) sets standards for Israeli institutions and recognition frameworks; foreign degree evaluation for olim sits with the Ministry as above. Verify which office currently handles your case.
- Link: https://www.gov.il/en/service/foreign-degree-evaluation-for-new-olim (could not be loaded by tool; verify) and NBN's summary: https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/employment/degrees-and-licensing/degree-recognition/

## Regulated professions (start early)

Licensing is separate from degree recognition and often requires exams, internships, or Hebrew. Check the government regulated-professions page (gov.il/en/pages/regulated_professions) and the relevant body:

| Profession | Authority / route | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Doctors, nurses, dentists, pharmacists, psychologists, paramedical | Ministry of Health (Misrad HaBriut) licensing; IMAP via NBN for medical professionals | Exams and Hebrew/clinical requirements vary; IMAP aims to speed licensure; online degrees often not recognized |
| Lawyers | Israel Bar Association (Lishkat Orchei HaDin) | Foreign-trained lawyers generally take Israeli bar exams after recognition steps; plan 1-2+ years; many work in international/English-language firms meanwhile |
| Accountants (CPA) | Accountants Council (Mo'etzet Ro'ei Cheshbon) at the Ministry of Justice | Exams for foreign CPAs; US CPAs can work in US-tax-focused roles meanwhile |
| Engineers and architects | Engineers and Architects Registrar (Pinkas HaMehandesim VeHaAdrichalim) | Registration by field; requires recognized degree and sometimes exams or experience; check current rules |
| Teachers | Ministry of Education | Recognition and certification, often Hebrew; native English teachers are in demand in English instruction |
| Social workers, other welfare professions | Ministry of Welfare registration | Check licensing |
| Real estate agents, appraisers, insurance agents | Ministries of Justice/Finance licensing | Exams in Hebrew |
| Electricians, plumbers, gas technicians, tradespeople | Ministry of Economy/Labor licensing and testing | Hebrew exams |

The "UNVERIFIED" caution applies to specifics (exam names, timing, fees): confirm on the official site or with NBN's professions index and the professional association.

## Other fields: market notes

- Healthcare: demand reported for nurses, doctors, dentists, therapists (Israel Hayom, Feb 2026), with IMAP supporting medical licensing.
- Education: native English tutors and teachers are needed, especially outside big Anglo communities; tutoring can supplement salary.
- Nonprofit/fundraising: English and donor-relations skills transfer; sector is large (about 5% of GDP per the same article).
- Sales: about 10% of vacancies nationwide are sales; English or other language skills help in international markets.
- Marketing/communications/content for English-speaking audiences has weakened (reported in 2025-2026); expect a tougher market.
- Construction/trades and appraisal: demand with certification.
- Finance/accounting: US tax and cross-border expertise has niche demand (NBN lists a US tax technician course).
- Academia/research: the Ministry's science-community integration support exists (verify at gov.il "assistance integrating into Israeli science community"); also look at the Israel Council for Higher Education and university postdoc programs.
