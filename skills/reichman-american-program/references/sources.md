# Sources and verification log

Last verified: November 2026 (research done 20 Nov 2026). Reichman's website is partly script-rendered, so several pages could only be confirmed through their linked PDFs or page titles. Items marked "unverified" were not confirmed from an official source. Always re-check the live page.

Names: the university was formerly IDC Herzliya; official domain is runi.ac.il (Reichman University). The English-taught international track is the Raphael Recanati International School (RRIS).
