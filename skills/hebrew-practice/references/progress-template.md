# Progress template and spaced review

The skill may not remember anything between sessions. At the end of a session Claude produces the block below, filled in; the learner saves it (notes app, document) and pastes it back at the start of the next session. Keep the block short. Do not include real names, addresses, ID numbers, or other private details.

## Spaced-review rules (Leitner-style, session-based)

Use session counts instead of dates when the learner does not provide dates.

| Box | Meaning | Review interval |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New mistake or just learned | next session |
| 2 | Correct once | after about 3 sessions or 1 week |
| 3 | Correct twice | after about 2 weeks |
| Mastered | Correct 3 times unprompted, in new sentences | monthly spot-check only |

- A miss (wrong or needed hints) moves the item back to Box 1.
- Review in new contexts rather than repeating the exact sentence: ask the learner to produce the phrase in a different scene ("now you are at the shuk and you want oat... something else").
- Review at most 3-5 items per session start so the session stays productive.
- Prioritize items by frequency (recurring mistake) over age.

## Starting a session with a pasted block

1. Read the profile; do not repeat intake. Confirm in one line ("Level 2, transliteration on, you're a woman, goal aliyah paperwork - correct?").
2. Run a 3-5 item review from the oldest or most frequent Box 1-2 items (new contexts).
3. Update boxes and counts based on results.
4. Then continue with a chosen format (quick-fire, role-play, describe-your-day, message drafting, comprehension, weekly review).

## Weekly review format

- Pull the top 3 recurring mistakes and test each in a fresh mini-scene (3 prompts).
- Re-check 3 "mastered" items to make sure they stuck.
- Show a before/after: one sentence the learner wrote early vs now.
- Produce a new block and suggest next week's focus.

## Wrap-up checklist for Claude

- Count mistakes by pattern across the session, not by word.
- List only patterns that appeared at least twice, or one clear high-value item.
- Give one specific praise (what improved).
- Suggest one audio step and one human step (see `resources.md`).
- Remind the learner to paste the block next time.
